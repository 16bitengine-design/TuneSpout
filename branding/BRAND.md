# TuneSpout brand guide

**Name:** TuneSpout  (assumed from the project name; confirm it, see "Before you publish")
**Tagline:** Sing, count & learn every day!
**Feeling:** sunny, musical, kind. Kids are the stars; the mascot is their friend.

## Mascot
**Spout** is a round blue whale-like friend with a blowhole that sprays music notes. He appears on the avatar, banner, watermark and TikTok cover. Kids stay the main characters in videos; invented sidekicks come and go, Spout is the only fixed character.

## Words
Short, warm, simple. Never scary, sarcastic or pushy. Headlines are two or three words with an exclamation mark ("LET'S ADD!").

## Before you publish
1. **Check the name and handle.** A web search found no existing "TuneSpout" brand, but that does not show the handles are free. Check YouTube and TikTok, and run a trademark search for your country before you invest in the name.
2. **Set the whole YouTube channel to "made for kids"** in YouTube Studio > Settings > Channel > Advanced settings. The uploader also marks each video.
3. **Verify the YouTube channel** (phone verification) or custom thumbnails will not upload.
4. **TikTok:** upload the avatar in the app. The API cannot change profile pictures.
